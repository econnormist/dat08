DATO‑808USB expander for Dato DRUM

No MIDI
–

Turn the Dato's own volume down, press Start and allow MIDI. Each Dato pad has 8 variants (◀ ▶ on the Dato) and each one is its own 808 sound here. The sliders bend that pad's pitch. Crush and Filter are applied here as you press them; Repeat, Random and Swing already come from the Dato. No Dato? Click the pads (or keys 1–4), the arrows, and hold Crush or Filter.
