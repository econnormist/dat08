Instrument keys · variation A–

Turn the Dato's own volume down, press Start and allow MIDI. Every Dato pad has 8 variants and each one is its own 808 sound here. The 16 keys play them: four per instrument, variations 1–4 on A and 5–8 on B; their lights follow the Dato. The Dato's sliders turn the Tune knobs. Crush and Filter are applied here (hold them on the Dato or turn their knobs); Repeat and Random come from the Dato itself. Knobs: drag up or down, scroll, or arrow keys; double-click resets.
